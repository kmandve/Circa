Under the hood

What the estimate is built from, and how much of it to believe.

{{ icon('info', 18) }}
This is a model of your body clock, not a measurement of it. The Fitbit Air has no light sensor and reports skin temperature only once a night, so the two strongest laboratory markers are never directly observed. Studies of similar wearables land within roughly 40–60 minutes of a real melatonin reading in regular sleepers, and much further out during travel or shift work. Circa reports that floor explicitly rather than hiding it.
